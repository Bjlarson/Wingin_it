06 Transparency & lighting rules

  • One light source: key light front-left at 315°. Every sheen, streak and bevel agrees with it.
  • Tint before blur: graphite at 55% opacity, 24px blur, 120% saturation. Never go under 45%, or text loses contrast over bright sky.
  • Contrast floor: primary text on the darkest expected backdrop must stay at 7:1 or better.
  • Reflections are static: streaks are baked into the panel and never animate. Only the selection rail moves.
  • Even rhythm: 56px rows, 36px side padding, 1px dividers at 5% white.
  • Depth: soft drop shadow only. No hard outline, no colored glass.
