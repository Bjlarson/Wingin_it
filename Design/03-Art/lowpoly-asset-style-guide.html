Rounded nose. Cowl and spinner are big and blunt. Never a sharp point.

3

Faceted canopy. Flat glass facets with one hard white glint. No reflections.

4

Team zone. Tail and wingtips are always team color. Skins can't paint over them.

5

3-tone flat shading. Top is light, side is the base color, belly is the shadow tone.

6

Bold outline. Inverted-hull outline on planes only, in ink #1E2433.

7

Tricycle gear only. One nose wheel plus two fat main wheels. The plane sits level, and the tail never gets a wheel.

8

Decal zone. Flat fuselage panel kept clear for roundels, numbers and stickers.

03 Facets & shading

Do
Flat normals and big readable facets. Shadows shift toward blue-violet. Corners get chunky chamfers.
Don't
Smooth gradients, photo textures, pure black shadows, tiny noisy facets or sharp 90° edges.

04 Modular parts · exploded

Five separate meshes, each with its own collider. Jagged broken-edge caps are hidden until a part detaches.

05 Damage states & FX

Smoke is made of chunky low-poly puffs, never particles that look like real smoke. In Damaged and Critical, damage smoke replaces cosmetic trails.
