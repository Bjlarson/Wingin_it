# Island Map

Status: Draft
Last updated: 2026-10-19

## Summary

One large island map with distinct biomes, each designed to encourage a different style of dogfight. Airfields are spread around it as repair hubs.

## Regions

### Central City

- Skyscrapers and bridges
- High-risk, close-quarters dogfights: weaving between towers and flying under bridges
- Good for skilled players and Fighters

### Mountain Range

- Natural cover, valleys, and canyons
- Ambush territory: break line of sight and lose missile locks

### Ocean

- Aircraft carriers (also used in Carrier War)
- Wide open, long-range engagements
- Good for Interceptors and missile play

### Desert

- Open air combat with few obstacles
- Clean, pure dogfights

### Airfields

- Safe repair zones
- AA guns at home airfields
- Placed between regions so every region has a "nearest" airfield

**Airfields** (same prefab as the test range, on flattened pads):

| Airfield | Position | Lands heading | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| West Home | (-1600, 0) | north | team 0 (the player), AA armed |
| East Home | (1600, 0) | south | team 1, AA armed |
| Central | (0, -700), south of the city | **either end** (two-way) | neutral (no AA, can't repair until capture exists) |

The two home fields are point-symmetric about the island center, so both teams have the same distance to the city (~1.6 km, ~19 s at Fighter cruise). Their approaches come in over open sea.

**Scale check:** coast to coast east-west is ~4.1 km = **~48 s** at Fighter cruise (85.5 m/s); the boundary circle is ~61 s across. Tested in `IslandSceneTests`.

### Retreat times (scale test, 2026-10-16)

Seconds at Fighter cruise (85.5 m/s), straight line, from each fight area to each airfield. **Bold** = that team's nearest field it can repair at today.

| Fight area | West Home (team 0) | East Home (team 1) | Central (neutral) |
|---|---|---|---|
| City center (0, 250) | **18.9** | **18.9** | 11.1 |
| The Spire | **16.3** | **21.6** | 11.5 |
| Summit Radar (mountains) | **13.1** | **29.6** | 19.8 |
| The Pyramid (desert) | **29.6** | **13.1** | 19.8 |
| Lighthouse Rock (ocean) | **29.5** | **29.5** | 14.6 |

What this means:

- **Each team's home region** (mountains for west, desert for east) is ~13 s from home, and the **city** ~19 s from either home. Both inside the 10–20 s rule.
- **The enemy's home region and the ocean** are ~30 s from your home field. Capturing **Central** brings every fight area to 11–20 s for the team that holds it, which is the reason to take it. Tested in `IslandSceneTests` (`EveryFightArea_Is10To20Seconds_FromAFriendlyAirfield_WithCentralCaptured`).
- **Fairness:** the city center and the ocean are the same distance from both home fields, and Summit Radar and the Pyramid are mirror images (same time from their own home). The Spire is the one exception: it stands west of the canal, so it's 5.3 s closer to West Home. The city center itself is even. Tested (`BothTeams_AreEquallyFarFromTheFights`).
- **No fight area under home flak:** every fight area is outside the 800 m reach of both home fields' AA guns (`NoFightArea_IsInsideAHomeAirfieldsAAUmbrella`).
- These are cruise times. A damaged plane is slower (Damaged/Critical lose 15% top speed, a dead engine glides), and boosting makes it shorter.

What changed in the scale test: the Pyramid moved from (1050, 120) to (804, 794), because it stood ~450 m from an East Home AA turret (inside team 1's flak) and only 6.6 s from East Home. The mesa that was there moved to (1150, 350). Lighthouse Rock moved from (700, -1850) to (0, -1950), because it was 10 s closer to East Home. Central moved 100 m north to (0, -700), so a captured Central covers the mountains and desert in under 20 s.

### Approach lanes (built, 2026-10-16)

Each home field has **three approach lanes**: marked routes from the fighting to the runway that all end on the field's **final approach fix**, a point on the glide path 600 m before the aim point (~31 m above the runway) and inside the home AA umbrella (2 of the 4 guns reach it). Each lane is an `ApproachLane` (a polyline of waypoints). Bots will fly these to retreat in Phase 5. Every waypoint is marked by a **gate**: two pylons 80 m apart, with no collision, in the lane's color. Waypoint heights are 50–60 m above the highest thing within 60 m, so the lanes run low over the sea and land.

Runways land one way only, so a lane from behind the field has to fly a circuit around it. That's why the lanes differ so much in length.

| Lane (West Home / East Home) | Color | Route | Length | Cruise time to the fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Canyon Run / Dune Run** | magenta | West: from the city through the mountain canyon, over the north end of the runway, out over the sea west of it, then a left downwind and base. East: the same circuit, but the first leg weaves low between the desert mesas | 3.4 km | ~40 s |
| **South Coast / North Shore** | cyan | From the ocean, low along the coast, then a straight-in final | 2.0 km | ~23 s |
| **Grassland** (both) | lime | From the city's edge toward Central, low over open ground, then a right base. East's version passes south of the Pyramid | 1.5 km | ~17 s |

- **Fairness:** East's lanes are West's mirrored through the island center. Where the terrain isn't a mirror image (no canyon in the desert, and the Pyramid sits where East's Grassland lane would be), East's lanes get their own points of the same length (within 1%).
- **Separate routes:** until 500 m from the fix, every lane stays at least 250 m from the other lanes into the same field, so one enemy can't cover two of them.
- **Debug HUD:** on a friendly lane (within 150 m of it), a diamond marks the next gate, labeled with the lane name and the distance left to the fix. The glide-path rings take over once you're on approach.
- **Tested** (`IslandSceneTests`): 2–3 lanes per home field, all ending on its glide path; every leg clear of terrain, buildings and landmarks with 25 m of room below; lanes kept apart; inside the boundary warning; the fix under home AA; both teams' lane lengths equal. An autopilot steering for each lane's next waypoint, the way a bot will, flies all six lanes without crashing or straying, and arrives on approach.
- **Central lands from either end (decided and built 2026-10-19).** An east-only runway would have sent every approach past West Home's AA (400–500 m from it, inside its 800 m reach), so team 1 would have had to land through team 0's flak. Central is now **two-way** (`Airfield.IsTwoWay`): the glide path, landing guide and HUD rings are mirrored at the far end, and the end is chosen by heading (`Airfield.ApproachesFromReverseEnd`; by which end is nearer when nearly stopped). Landing, braking and repair don't care about direction. It has **one lane into each end** (`West Final`, `East Final`, gold gates): the same route from the south sea, mirrored across x = 0, ending 600 m out on that end's glide path, so both teams and both ends get identical approaches, clear of both homes' AA. Tested: `Central_IsTwoWay_WithOneLaneIntoEachEnd`, the shared lane tests, and the autopilot flies both. Bots don't pick a Central end yet (they only land at friendly fields, and Central has no owner until Domination capture exists).
- **Needs a hands-on flight:** is the 40 s Canyon Run worth flying, compared to the 17 s Grassland lane? Are the gates readable at speed?

**Spawn to fight:** the air spawn is 300 m up and 250 m behind each home runway, facing down the runway (north for west, south for east), not toward the center. The city center is ~1.7 km (~20 s) away, and two teams flying at each other close to missile lock range (900 m) after ~14 s. That misses the "find a fight within ~10 s" target (see Scale below). Fixes to consider later: point the air spawns at the center, or spawn further forward.

### Clouds (built, 2026-10-16)

Twelve puffy **cloud banks** at mid-altitude give cover. Each is a `CloudBank`: a flat core puff, a ring of 6–8 side puffs and two on top, all ellipsoids. The rendered white spheres match the cover volume exactly. Clouds have **no colliders**: planes, bullets, rockets and bombs go straight through them.

- **What a cloud blocks** (`CloudCover`): a sight line that crosses **40 m or more** of cloud is blocked. That breaks missile locks (building or holding), makes a missile in flight lose its target for good (the same as a shake-off), stops home AA from tracking you, and hides your plane's bracket, pipper and readout on an enemy's HUD. A plane just inside the edge is still visible; ~40 m in, it's hidden from every side. Two planes in the same cloud less than 40 m apart can still see each other: a knife fight in the fog.
- **What it doesn't block:** guns. You can still spray into a cloud at where you think someone is. **Support radar sees through clouds**, which gives the Support a reason to fly with the team.
- **Layout:** six banks on the west half, each mirrored through the island center for the east (like the approach lanes), so both teams get the same cover. They hang over the fight areas: between the city and the mountains, south city toward Central, both coasts, and the seas toward the lighthouse. Bank radius 190–240 m, ~150 m thick, bases at **335–430 m** (tops 500–580 m). The pair between the city and the mountains is lifted to a 560 m base (top ~720 m) to clear the peaks: the underside is always 80+ m above anything below. A bank and its mirror share one altitude.
- **Kept clear:** no cloud reaches into home AA range (or attackers could camp a field from inside one), covers a landmark top, hides a landmark from mid-map, sits on an air spawn, or crosses an approach lane.
- **Feedback:** the screen washes out white the deeper the camera is in cloud. A label says **IN CLOUD: HIDDEN FROM LOCKS AND AA** when you're deep enough, or **CLOUD EDGE: STILL VISIBLE**.
- **Tested** (`IslandSceneTests`, `CloudCoverTests`, plus cloud cases in the missile and AA tests): mirrored pairs; the middle of every bank hides a plane; 60 m+ of room under every puff; no cloud in home AA range; every fight area has a cloud within 10 s at Fighter cruise; landmarks, spawns and lanes clear. Missile locks fail through 120 m of cloud but not through a 30 m wisp; a missile loses a target that flies into cloud; AA never targets an enemy circling inside one.
- **Later:** drifting clouds, real volumetric art (Phase 11), and bots using clouds to break locks and hide (Phase 5: `CloudCover.CanSee` / `IsHidden` are ready).
- **Needs a hands-on flight:** is 40 m the right depth? Are the clouds too easy a missile escape? Is the whiteout too blinding to fight in?

### Boundary (built, 2026-10-16)

The edge is a circle 2600 m from the island center, all over open sea (the nearest land, Lighthouse Rock, is 650 m inside it).

- **Seen:** a ring of **109 marker buoys** floats on the sea at the edge, ~150 m apart: an orange float, a white mast and a red light 28 m up, all blinking together so the ring reads as one dotted line (`BoundaryBuoys`). No colliders: they mark the edge, they aren't in the way.
- **Warned:** from 2300 m out the HUD shows COMBAT AREA EDGE, then LEAVING COMBAT AREA — TURNING BACK past the edge. Near the 1500 m ceiling: ALTITUDE LIMIT.
- **Turned back:** past the edge, the same autopilot bots will use takes the stick over 0.6 s and steers for the island center, holding altitude (at least 150 m, below the ceiling band). It lets go once you're back inside. Throttle and boost stay yours. Over the ceiling, it noses the plane down.
- **Tested** (`IslandSceneTests`): every class, on 8 bearings, at 30 / 600 / 1400 m, flying straight out or along the edge, hands off or boosting outward against it. All are back inside in **3–6 s**, at most **~280 m** past the edge (the Interceptor boosting straight out), never lose height, and hands off they don't drift back out once it lets go.

### Mode arenas (built, 2026-10-19)

Modes that need a different play area get an arena on this map instead of a new scene (`Mode Arenas` in the scene, `ModeArena`; rules in [match-modes](../01-Game-Design/match-modes.md#arenas-built-2026-10-19)). Only **Ram Royale** has one so far: a 700 m circle round Lighthouse Rock with a 450 m ceiling and a hard edge, every airfield closed, an air start on a 550 m ring. While it's in force the buoy ring moves to its edge (~30 buoys). Its north edge just reaches the south coast; the rest is open sea.

## Design Rules

- **Retreat distance:** from any point of action, a friendly airfield should be roughly **10–20 seconds** of flight away (proposed). Matches are only ~5 minutes, so a long retreat takes too much of the match. Too close removes the risk.
- **Approach lanes:** each airfield should have 2–3 approach routes, so retreating players can't be trivially intercepted. (Built for the home fields: see [Approach lanes](#approach-lanes-built-2026-10-16).)
- **Landmarks:** each region needs a big landmark visible from far away for navigation. (Built: see the table above.)
- **Vertical space:** set a ceiling altitude. Clouds at mid-altitude give cover. (Built: see [Clouds](#clouds-built-2026-10-16).)
- **Boundary:** an ocean edge with a soft turn-back. (Built: see [Boundary](#boundary-built-2026-10-16).)

## Scale

Sized for **up to 16 players** and **~5-minute matches**. With 16 planes, a big map feels empty fast, so keep it compact. Starting point: the map should be crossable in **~45–60 seconds** at a Fighter's cruise speed (proposed). Players should find a fight within ~10 seconds of spawning.

Teams mode airfields (proposed): 2 home airfields + 1 central neutral, capturable airfield.
