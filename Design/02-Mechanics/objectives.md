# Objectives

Status: Draft
Last updated: 2026-10-13

## Summary

Objectives give players XP and team benefits without needing to win duels. They spread fights across the map and stop matches from turning into pure kill-chasing.

## Objective Types

### AI Convoys

- Ground vehicles moving along roads between regions
- Destroy for XP (bombs and rockets are effective)
- **Proposed:** convoys have light AA fire, so attacking them isn't free

### Supply Drops

- Spawn periodically around the map, announced to all players
- Contents (proposed): ammo restock, a temporary buff, or a small heal, plus bonus XP
- Creates hotspots where players converge to fight

### Radar Stations

- Capture by flying low through a capture zone, or by destroying the enemy station and holding the area
- Gives the owning team map vision (enemy icons on the minimap within a radius)

### Airfield Capture

- Neutral or enemy airfields can be captured
- Team-wide benefits:
  - A forward repair point
  - A forward spawn point (proposed)
  - Passive team score trickle (proposed)
- Home airfields cannot be captured (proposed)

## Capture Mechanic (proposed)

- Fly within the zone's cylinder below an altitude ceiling
- More friendly planes inside = faster capture
- An enemy plane inside = contested (no progress)
