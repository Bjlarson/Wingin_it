## Sky Racing Prototype (offline) *(moved out of Phase 6.6, 2026-10-25; design decided 2026-10-20)*

📄 [match-modes](01-Game-Design/match-modes.md#sky-racing-prototype) · [weapons](02-Mechanics/weapons.md) · [health-and-damage](02-Mechanics/health-and-damage.md) · [bots](02-Mechanics/bots.md)

A fifth mode in the Diddy Kong Racing / Mario Kart mold, in **its own scene with a purpose-built track**: fly a lap course through gates, fly through **colored mystery boxes** for an item, and use it on the pack. Not started. Parked for a future update: it needs the Phase 6.5 mode framework (built) and a go-ahead.

**Decided (2026-10-20):** new scene + track (a **curvy closed loop**, lapped) · 3 items only (boost, missile, shield) · guns and bombs off, items are the only weapons · **real part damage** applies · **only a crash** respawns you (within **3 s**, near where you were in the race); losing a wing or other part does not · one plane (the main plane, Fighter) for every racer, told apart by **wingtip color** · **no weighted items**; **homing missile is built first**, then boost and shield; box color sets the item (Diddy Kong style): **blue = boost, red = missile, green = shield**.

**Scene & course**
- [ ] `Race` scene generated by a scene builder (like `IslandSceneBuilder`): terrain/scenery, a closed loop track of gate rings, a start/finish line, a grid start
- [ ] `RaceCourse`: ordered gates (`Checkpoint`), lap count, pure progress math (`RaceBoard`: lap, next gate, wrong-way and skipped-gate checks, live position by lap + gate + distance to next gate), EditMode-tested
- [ ] Curvy, circular lap (smooth bends, some banking-style S-curves, no long dead straights); racers up to 8, or fewer if the track is too tight to fit them (decide from the grid size); track length about 2–3 min a lap at Fighter cruise: fast straights, weaving sections, dips; clear of terrain so a mistake costs time, not a crash every lap

**Mode rules**
- [ ] `RaceMode` on the `MatchMode` framework: 3-2-1 grid start, 3 laps, finishing order, placement points, time cap, game mode card with a race sketch
- [ ] Free-for-all, up to 8 racers, however many fit the track (bots fill); all fly the Fighter; wingtip color per racer (the player's choice later via cosmetics)
- [ ] Guns, rockets and bombs off (`OnSpawned` loadout hook); no repair, no airfields
- [ ] **Respawn on a crash only** (terrain, water, obstacle, destroyed): back on the course near where you were (last gate passed, facing the next, **up to 3 s** delay), fully repaired. A lost wing, stabilizer or engine stays lost and you race on damaged
- [ ] Mid-race damage rules from items: missile = normal missile damage

**Boxes & items**
- [ ] `MysteryBox` pickups in three colors placed along the course; fly through to take the item, one held at a time, box respawns after a few seconds
- [ ] **Red = Homing Missile** first (the existing `PlaneMissiles` lock-on, warning and evade, unchanged), then **Blue = Boost** (a long burst) and **Green = Shield** (Armor Shield bubble)
- [ ] Item slot + use button (secondary fire / ability on PC and gamepad, touch button)

**Bots, HUD, polish**
- [ ] Bots: follow a racing line through the gates, pick boxes, use items (missile at the plane ahead, shield when a missile is inbound, boost on straights), recover after a respawn
- [ ] HUD: position (3 / 8), lap, next-gate arrow and marker, held item, minimap with the track, lap times, wrong-way warning
- [ ] Race results screen: finishing order, best lap

✅ **Done when:** a 3-lap race against bots is playable on PC and mobile without dev tools, items change who wins, and the playtest decides if Sky Racing ships.
